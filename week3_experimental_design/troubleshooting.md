# Troubleshooting and Risk Management

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

| Problem | Possible Cause | Detection | Corrective Action |
|---------|----------------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Recruitment Challenges** | Strict exclusion criteria (e.g., excluding past depression). | Weekly enrollment reports lagging behind targets. | Broaden advertising scope; engage additional clinical sites; review exclusion criteria if overly restrictive. |
| **High Attrition / Dropout** | High burden of study visits; GI side effects of Semaglutide. | Monthly retention tracking shows >20% loss to follow-up. | Increase patient contact via phone/text; offer flexibility in visit scheduling; aggressive management of GI symptoms. |
| **Protocol Deviations** | Staff turnover at sites; unclear instructions. | Identified during central monitoring and source data verification. | Re-training of site staff; simplify protocol guidelines; issue clarification memos. |
| **Missing Psychiatric Assessments** | Time constraints during visits; patient fatigue. | Empty fields in REDCap eCRFs. | Implement forced-response fields on digital tablets; prioritize psychiatric questionnaires at the start of visits. |
| **Unblinding Events** | Predictable weight loss/GI side effects of Semaglutide revealing assignment. | Patient or physician reports guessing the treatment arm. | Rely on objective, patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) less susceptible to clinician bias; document all suspected unblinding. |
| **PHQ-9 Ceiling/Floor Effects** | Population may have too low baseline depression for subtle changes to register. | Baseline data analysis shows highly skewed distributions. | Ensure adequate sample size to detect small shifts; utilize GAD-7 and ISI to capture alternative manifestations of distress. |
| **Concomitant Medication Changes** | Primary care physicians prescribing antidepressants off-protocol. | Medication reconciliation at follow-up visits. | Prompt reporting to PI; analyze as a time-varying covariate; potential transition to per-protocol analysis subset. |
| **Site Non-compliance** | Lack of PI oversight; under-resourced site. | Routine auditing reveals incomplete informed consents or missing labs. | Issue formal warning; halt enrollment at specific site; extreme cases require site closure and patient transfer. |
| **Data Entry Errors** | Manual transcription from paper to electronic systems. | Range checks and data validation rules in REDCap fire frequently. | Enforce direct electronic data capture by patients; mandate double data entry for critical endpoints. |
| **Unexpected Safety Signals** | Rare severe adverse events (e.g., pancreatitis). | SAE reports received within 24 hours. | Immediate DSMB review; halt trial if stopping rules are met; notify regulatory authorities. |
