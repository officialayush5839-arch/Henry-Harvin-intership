# Ethical Considerations

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Regulatory Approvals
- **IRB/Ethics Committee:** Full approval from a central Institutional Review Board (IRB) and local ethics committees at all participating sites is required prior to any study activities.

## Informed Consent
- **Process:** Consent must be obtained voluntarily and without coercion.
- **Content:** The consent form will explicitly detail the theoretical risk of mood changes and psychiatric side effects. It will clearly state the benefits, risks, alternatives to participation, and the unequivocal right to withdraw from the study at any time without penalty or loss of standard medical care.

## Data Privacy and Security
- **HIPAA Compliance:** All patient data will be handled in strict accordance with HIPAA (or equivalent regional privacy laws).
- **De-identification:** Data extracted for statistical analysis will be de-identified, utilizing study-specific IDs rather than PHI (Protected Health Information).
- **Secure Storage:** All physical documents will be stored in double-locked facilities. Electronic data will reside on encrypted, access-controlled servers.

## Vulnerable Populations
- **Psychiatric Risk:** Patients with a history of severe psychiatric illness are excluded to protect them from potential harm. However, patients developing new symptoms during the trial become a vulnerable population requiring special protections, swift intervention, and guaranteed psychiatric care as outlined in the safety protocol.

## Conflict of Interest
- All investigators and site staff must declare any financial or professional conflicts of interest. The DSMB must be entirely independent.

## The 3Rs Principles
- While the 3Rs (Replacement, Reduction, Refinement) typically apply to animal research, a parallel ethical framework applies here: reducing the sample size to the minimum needed for adequate power, and refining protocols to minimize patient burden and risk.

## Participant Compensation and Post-Trial Access
- **Compensation:** Participants will receive fair compensation for travel and time associated with study visits, prorated based on completion.
- **Post-Trial Access:** The sponsor will ensure that patients benefiting from their assigned therapy will have a pathway to access the treatment following the conclusion of the trial.
