# Sample Design

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Target Population
- **Demographics:** Adults aged 18-75 years.
- **Clinical Indication:** Diagnosed with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) or clinical obesity (BMI >= 30 kg/m²).
- **Treatment Status:** Initiating new antidiabetic or anti-obesity therapy (naive to both GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibitors for at least 6 months prior).

## Sample Size and Power Calculation
- **Total Sample Size:** N = 2,000 (1,000 per treatment arm).
- **Parameters:** 
  - Alpha level = 0.05
  - Statistical Power = 0.80 (80%)
  - Estimated baseline psychiatric incidence in standard population = 5.0%
  - Hypothesized incidence in Semaglutide group = 8.5%
  - Anticipated Hazard Ratio (HR) ~ 1.70.
- **Attrition Adjustment:** The sample size accounts for an estimated 18.4% attrition rate over the 12-month follow-up period.

## Sampling Strategy
- **Sites:** Multicenter study involving 10-15 clinical sites across diverse geographic regions to ensure a representative sample.
- **Randomization:** 1:1 centralized, concealed allocation. 
- **Stratification:** Stratified by biological sex, age group (18-45, 46-60, 61-75), and baseline PHQ-9 score category (0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild).

## Inclusion & Exclusion Criteria
**Inclusion:**
- Willing and able to provide informed consent.
- Indication for Semaglutide or SGLT2 inhibitor.
- Baseline PHQ-9 < 10 (no active moderate/severe depression).

**Exclusion:**
- History of major depressive disorder, bipolar disorder, schizophrenia, or active suicidal ideation in the past 5 years.
- Current use of antidepressants, antipsychotics, or mood stabilizers.
- Known contraindications to GLP-1 RAs or SGLT2 inhibitors.
