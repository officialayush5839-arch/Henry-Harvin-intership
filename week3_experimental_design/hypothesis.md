# Hypotheses

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Primary Hypotheses

**Null Hypothesis (H0):** The cumulative incidence of new-onset psychiatric disorders (measured by diagnostic thresholds on the PHQ-9 and GAD-7) over a 12-month follow-up period is equal between the Semaglutide and SGLT2 inhibitor treatment groups.

**Alternative Hypothesis (H1):** The cumulative incidence of new-onset psychiatric disorders over a 12-month follow-up period is significantly higher in the Semaglutide group compared to the SGLT2 inhibitor group.

## Secondary Hypotheses

**Secondary Hypothesis 1 (Dose-Response):** 
- **H0:** There is no difference in the incidence of psychiatric adverse events between patients receiving standard T2DM doses of Semaglutide and those receiving higher weight-management doses.
- **H1:** Higher doses of Semaglutide (e.g., 2.4 mg/week) are associated with a significantly higher incidence of psychiatric adverse events compared to lower doses (e.g., 1.0 mg/week).

**Secondary Hypothesis 2 (Temporal Patterns):**
- **H0:** The onset of new psychiatric symptoms is uniformly distributed throughout the 12-month study period.
- **H1:** The onset of new psychiatric symptoms in the Semaglutide group clusters disproportionately during the first 3 months of therapy (the dose-titration phase).
