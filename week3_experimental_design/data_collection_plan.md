# Data Collection Plan

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Instruments and Metrics
1. **Clinical Data:** Height, weight (BMI calculation), blood pressure, heart rate.
2. **Laboratory Data:** HbA1c, fasting glucose, comprehensive metabolic panel (liver and kidney function), lipid panel.
3. **Psychiatric Questionnaires (Patient-Reported Outcomes):**
   - PHQ-9 (Depression)
   - GAD-7 (Anxiety)
   - C-SSRS (Suicide Severity)
   - ISI (Insomnia Severity Index)

## Quality Control Procedures
- All questionnaire data will ideally be collected directly from patients via secure tablets in the waiting room to minimize transcription errors.
- Electronic Case Report Forms (eCRFs) in REDCap will have forced-choice fields and range checks.
- Missing data prompts will alert coordinators before a patient leaves the clinic.
