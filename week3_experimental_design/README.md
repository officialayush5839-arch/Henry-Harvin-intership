# Week 3 Experimental Design Simulation: Semaglutide Psychiatric Safety Prospective Study

**Disclaimer: This is a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design created for a pharmaceutical research portfolio. It is NOT an executed experiment.**

## Overview
This folder contains the proposed experimental design for a prospective cohort study aiming to validate the pharmacovigilance signal (identified in Week 2 via FAERS data) suggesting a potential association between Semaglutide use and new-onset psychiatric adverse events (such as major depressive disorder and generalized anxiety disorder).

## Objectives
- To design a rigorous prospective study comparing the incidence of psychiatric disorders in patients initiating Semaglutide versus an active comparator (SGLT2 inhibitors).
- To establish clear clinical protocols, variables, controls, and ethical boundaries for monitoring psychiatric safety in metabolic drug trials.

## Design Rationale
A prospective cohort design with an active comparator was chosen to minimize confounding by indication and better isolate the independent effects of Semaglutide from the psychological impact of living with Type 2 Diabetes or Obesity, or the general psychological impact of weight loss.
