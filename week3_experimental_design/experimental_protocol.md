# Experimental Protocol

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## 1. Screening and Eligibility Assessment (Weeks -2 to 0)
Potential participants will undergo a 2-week screening period. Clinical investigators will review medical history, conduct physical examinations, and assess inclusion/exclusion criteria. Baseline laboratory values (HbA1c, renal function, liver function) will be obtained. 

## 2. Baseline Psychiatric Assessment (Week 0)
Prior to randomization, eligible participants will complete baseline psychiatric evaluations:
- **PHQ-9:** Patient Health Questionnaire-9 for depressive symptoms.
- **GAD-7:** Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 for anxiety symptoms.
- **C-SSRS:** Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale to assess baseline suicidal ideation.
- **ISI:** Insomnia Severity Index to capture sleep disturbances, a common prodromal psychiatric symptom.

## 3. Randomization
Participants will be randomized in a 1:1 ratio to either the Semaglutide arm or the SGLT2 inhibitor arm. Randomization will be managed via a centralized, secure web-based system to ensure concealed allocation. Stratification will occur based on biological sex, age group, and baseline PHQ-9 scores.

## 4. Treatment Initiation and Titration
- **Semaglutide Arm:** Initiated at 0.25 mg subcutaneously once weekly for 4 weeks, followed by standard dose escalation protocols based on the clinical indication (T2DM vs. Obesity) and patient tolerability.
- **SGLT2 Inhibitor Arm:** Initiated at the standard starting dose for the specific drug chosen (e.g., Empagliflozin 10 mg daily), with adjustment as clinically indicated.

## 5. Follow-up Visits
Structured clinical follow-up visits will occur at Month 1, Month 3, Month 6, Month 9, and Month 12. 

## 6. Psychiatric Assessments During Follow-up
At every designated follow-up visit (Months 1, 3, 6, 9, 12), the full battery of psychiatric assessments (PHQ-9, GAD-7, C-SSRS) will be re-administered. Any patient scoring >9 on the PHQ-9 or GAD-7, or expressing *any* suicidal ideation on the C-SSRS, will immediately trigger the Safety Protocol.

## 7. Biomarker and PK Sampling
Blood samples will be drawn at Baseline, Month 3, and Month 12 to assess metabolic parameters (HbA1c, lipid profiles), renal function, and potentially inflammatory markers hypothesized to link metabolic changes to CNS effects.

## 8. Adverse Event Documentation
Standard safety reporting will occur continuously. Non-psychiatric adverse events (e.g., gastrointestinal distress) will be systematically recorded to assess whether physical tolerability correlates with psychiatric distress.

## 9. Quality Control
- **Monitoring:** Centralized statistical monitoring will look for site-specific data anomalies.
- **Source Data Verification:** 10% of all electronic Case Report Forms (eCRFs) will undergo rigorous source data verification by independent clinical research associates.

## 10. Data Management
All clinical and survey data will be captured using REDCap (Research Electronic Data Capture). To ensure high data integrity, critical endpoints will utilize double data entry protocols, and the system will include automated logic checks for out-of-range values.

## 11. Statistical Analysis Approach
The primary analysis will be conducted under the Intention-To-Treat (ITT) principle. Survival analysis techniques, specifically Kaplan-Meier curves and Cox Proportional Hazards regression, will be used to evaluate the time to onset of psychiatric outcomes, adjusting for the stratification variables.

## 12. Interim Analysis
A pre-planned interim analysis will be conducted by an independent Data Safety Monitoring Board (DSMB) when 50% of the target sample has completed their 6-month assessment. An O'Brien-Fleming stopping boundary will be employed to evaluate for overwhelming efficacy or, more critically in this context, unacceptable harm (significant excess psychiatric events).
