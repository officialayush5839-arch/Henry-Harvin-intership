# Research Question

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Primary Research Question
Does Semaglutide cause a significantly higher incidence of new-onset psychiatric disorders (specifically, major depressive disorder and generalized anxiety disorder) compared to SGLT2 inhibitors in adults with Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM) or obesity over a 12-month follow-up period?

## Secondary Research Questions
1. **Dose-Response:** Is there a dose-dependent relationship between Semaglutide (e.g., standard T2DM dosing up to 1.0 mg/week vs. obesity dosing up to 2.4 mg/week) and the incidence or severity of psychiatric adverse events?
2. **Temporal Onset Patterns:** What is the median time to onset of new psychiatric symptoms following the initiation of Semaglutide compared to SGLT2 inhibitors? Does risk cluster during the dose-titration phase?
3. **Vulnerability Factors:** Do baseline demographic or clinical characteristics (such as a history of subclinical mood disturbances, baseline BMI, or concurrent life stressors) modify the risk of developing psychiatric disorders while on Semaglutide?
