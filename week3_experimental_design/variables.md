# Study Variables

**Disclaimer: This document outlines a SIMULATED/PROPOSED experimental design for educational and portfolio purposes. It is NOT an executed clinical trial.**

## Independent Variable
- **Drug Exposure:** The primary independent variable is the pharmacological treatment assigned to the patient.
  - *Group A:* Semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist)
  - *Group B:* SGLT2 Inhibitor (active comparator)

## Dependent Variable
- **Psychiatric Incidence:** The incidence of new-onset psychiatric disorders over the 12-month follow-up. 
  - *Measurement Instruments:* Patient Health Questionnaire-9 (PHQ-9) for depression, Generalized Anxiety Disorder-7 (GAD-7) for anxiety, and the Columbia-Suicide Severity Rating Scale (C-SSRS) for suicidal ideation/behavior.

## Control Variables
To isolate the effect of the drug exposure, the following variables will be strictly measured and controlled for via stratification and multivariate analysis:
- **Demographics:** Age, biological sex.
- **Biometrics:** Baseline Body Mass Index (BMI).
- **Clinical Baseline:** Baseline psychiatric status (patients with active severe psychiatric illness are excluded, but subclinical baseline scores are recorded).
- **Medical History:** Concomitant medications, known comorbidities.
- **Lifestyle Factors:** Smoking status, alcohol use.

## Confounding Variables
Potential confounders that require careful analytic handling:
- **Obesity-Depression Comorbidity:** The complex bidirectional relationship between metabolic syndrome/obesity and depression.
- **Weight Loss Effects:** Significant rapid weight loss can inherently alter mood and psychological well-being independent of the pharmacological mechanism of the drug.
- **Lifestyle Changes:** Diet and exercise modifications mandated or encouraged alongside antidiabetic/weight-loss therapies can impact mental health.
