# Citation Style Guide

## Standard

This project uses **APA 7th Edition** citation style throughout all documents, reports, and references.

## In-Text Citations

### Single Author
- (Author, Year)
- Author (Year)

### Two Authors
- (Author1 & Author2, Year)
- Author1 and Author2 (Year)

### Three or More Authors
- (First Author et al., Year)
- First Author et al. (Year)

### Direct Quotes
- (Author, Year, p. XX)

### Multiple Citations
- (Author1, Year; Author2, Year) — alphabetical order

## Reference List Format

### Journal Article
Author, A. A., Author, B. B., & Author, C. C. (Year). Title of article. *Title of Periodical*, *volume*(issue), page–page. https://doi.org/xxxxx

### Journal Article with DOI
Wilding, J. P. H., Batterham, R. L., Calanna, S., Davies, M., Van Gaal, L. F., Lingvay, I., ... & Kushner, R. F. (2021). Once-weekly semaglutide in adults with overweight or obesity. *New England Journal of Medicine*, *384*(11), 989–1002. https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183

### Online Database/Report
U.S. Food and Drug Administration. (2024). *FDA Adverse Event Reporting System (FAERS) quarterly data files*. https://www.fda.gov/drugs/questions-and-answers-fdas-adverse-event-reporting-system-faers

## DOI Formatting

- Always include DOI when available
- Format as: https://doi.org/xxxxx
- Do not include a period after the DOI

## PMID

- Include PubMed ID (PMID) in the literature matrix and CSV reference files for traceability
- PMID is not required in APA formatted reference lists but is included in this project for verification purposes

## Special Notes

1. All references must be verifiable — no fabricated citations
2. Every in-text citation must have a corresponding reference entry
3. Every reference entry must be cited at least once (unless marked as background reading)
4. Author names must be spelled consistently across all documents
5. Publication years must match between in-text citations and references
