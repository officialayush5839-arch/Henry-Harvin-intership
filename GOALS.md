# Project Goals

## Primary Goal

Create a complete, reproducible, four-week pharmaceutical research portfolio demonstrating competency in literature review, public-data analysis, experimental design simulation, and methodological critical evaluation.

## Research Goal

Investigate the post-marketing safety profile of Semaglutide (GLP-1 receptor agonist) with specific focus on psychiatric adverse drug reactions using the FDA FAERS database.

## Weekly Goals

### Week 1 — Literature Review
- Conduct a systematic review of 10–20 recent scholarly articles on Semaglutide pharmacovigilance
- Identify emerging trends, breakthroughs, challenges, and research gaps
- Formulate a research question and testable hypothesis
- Produce a professionally formatted literature review report

### Week 2 — Data Collection and Statistical Analysis
- Obtain and document a public pharmaceutical dataset (FDA FAERS)
- Perform data cleaning, descriptive statistics, and disproportionality analysis
- Compute Reporting Odds Ratio (ROR), Proportional Reporting Ratio (PRR), and Chi-square tests
- Generate scientific visualizations and a comprehensive data analysis report

### Week 3 — Experimental Design Simulation
- Design a prospective active surveillance cohort study
- Define variables, controls, sample design, protocol, safety, and ethics
- Create experimental workflow flowcharts
- Produce a complete experimental design report

### Week 4 — Critical Evaluation
- Select and critically evaluate a peer-reviewed experimental article (STEP 1 Trial)
- Compare methodology against best-practice expectations
- Create a methodology comparison matrix
- Produce a critical evaluation report

## Success Criteria

| Criterion | Target |
|-----------|--------|
| Scholarly articles verified | ≥ 10 |
| Public dataset documented | 1 (FDA FAERS) |
| Statistical analyses completed | ≥ 5 methods |
| Scientific figures generated | ≥ 8 |
| Word reports produced | 5 (4 weekly + 1 final) |
| Submission descriptions | 4 × 200+ words |
| QA checklists completed | 6 |
| No fabricated evidence | Mandatory |
| Reproducible analysis pipeline | Mandatory |
| Cross-week continuity | Mandatory |
