## Research Foundation Summary

**Primary RQ:** Is Semaglutide associated with a disproportionately higher reporting rate of psychiatric adverse events (depression, suicidal ideation) compared to SGLT2 inhibitors in the FDA FAERS database (2018–2025)?

**Hypothesis:**
- H₀: ROR ≤ 1.0 (no disproportionate signal)
- H₁: ROR > 1.0 (disproportionate signal)

**Primary Objective:** Quantify the disproportionality of psychiatric ADR reporting for Semaglutide vs SGLT2 inhibitors using ROR and PRR.

**Secondary Objectives:**
1. Characterize the most frequently reported psychiatric ADR categories
2. Analyze temporal trends in psychiatric ADR reporting (2018–2025)
3. Compare reporting patterns across Semaglutide formulations
4. Evaluate demographic patterns in psychiatric ADR reports
5. Compare FAERS findings with controlled trial evidence
