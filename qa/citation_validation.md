## 1. Audit Scope & Methodology

This audit conducted an exhaustive verification of all bibliographic sources, in-text citations, and metadata across all reports, markdown documents, and data matrices in the research portfolio. Every source was evaluated against:
1. **Verifiability**: Verified existence in the National Library of Medicine (PubMed/MEDLINE), Crossref, or official regulatory registers.
2. **DOI & PMID Accuracy**: Validated alphanumeric Digital Object Identifiers and PubMed IDs.
3. **No-Hallucination Gate**: Zero fabricated, synthetic, or phantom citations allowed.
4. **Citation-Reference Concordance**: Verification that every in-text citation corresponds to an entry in `references/master_references.bib` and `references/master_references.csv`.
