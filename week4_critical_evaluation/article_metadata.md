# Article Metadata

**Title:** Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity
**Authors:** John P.H. Wilding, Rachel L. Batterham, Salvatore Calanna, Melanie Davies, Luc F. Van Gaal, Ildiko Lingvay, Barbara M. McGowan, Julio Rosenstock, Marie T.D. Tran, Thomas A. Wadden, Sean Wharton, Koutaro Yokote, Niels Zeuthen, Robert F. Kushner, for the STEP 1 Study Group
**Journal:** New England Journal of Medicine
**Year of Publication:** 2021
**Volume:** 384
**Issue:** 11
**Pages:** 989-1002
**DOI:** 10.1056/NEJMoa2032183
**PMID:** 33567185
**ClinicalTrials.gov Identifier:** NCT03548935
**Sponsor:** Novo Nordisk
**Trial Registration:** ClinicalTrials.gov
**Study Design:** Phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicenter, multinational trial
**Primary Endpoints:** 
1. Percentage change in body weight from baseline to week 68
2. Proportion of participants achieving a weight loss of 5% or more from baseline to week 68
**Key Result:** Participants receiving semaglutide achieved a mean weight change of -14.9% compared to -2.4% with placebo.
