# Week 4: Critical Evaluation of Research Literature

## Overview
This directory contains the critical evaluation of a selected piece of scientific literature relevant to the pharmaceutical research portfolio on Semaglutide safety and efficacy. The objective of Week 4 is to systematically analyze a landmark randomized controlled trial (RCT), assessing its methodology, strengths, limitations, and adherence to best practices.

## Objectives
- Select and justify a primary research article relevant to the portfolio's focus.
- Extract and document complete metadata for the selected article.
- Thoroughly summarize the methodology employed in the study.
- Systematically evaluate the strengths of the study design and execution.
- Identify and critically analyze the limitations of the study.
- Propose actionable recommendations for improving future studies in this domain.
- Compare the study's reporting and methodology against established best practice standards (CONSORT, ICH-GCP, Cochrane RoB 2).

## Selected Article
The selected article for this week's critical evaluation is the STEP 1 trial:
**Title:** Once-Weekly Semaglutide in Adults with Overweight or Obesity
**Authors:** Wilding JPH, Batterham RL, Calanna S, et al.
**Journal:** New England Journal of Medicine, 2021.

This pivotal Phase 3 trial was chosen due to its foundational role in establishing the efficacy and safety profile of semaglutide 2.4 mg (Wegovy) for weight management, providing a robust dataset to evaluate against the post-marketing real-world data explored in earlier weeks of this portfolio.
