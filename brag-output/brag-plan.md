# Brag Plan: Semaglutide Post-Marketing Neuropsychiatric Safety Portfolio

## What is this app?
An enterprise-grade pharmaceutical research portfolio investigating post-marketing neuropsychiatric safety signals of Semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy) across 148,178 FDA FAERS records with a prospective active-surveillance cohort simulation and clinical trial critique.

## The angle
Big Pharma meets Big Data: resolving the high-stakes global medical controversy of whether GLP-1 receptor agonists cause psychiatric adverse reactions through rigorous computational pharmacovigilance and trial deconstruction.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
Cinematic shot of a holographic GLP-1 molecular laboratory with high-contrast text:
**"DOES SEMAGLUTIDE CAUSE DEPRESSION?"**
Fast transition into: **"A $100B drug. A global safety paradox."**

## Key moments (the middle)
1. **The Big Data Mining (3-7s)**:
   - Visual: Cyber-command center with 148,178 FDA FAERS adverse event records streaming across monitors.
   - Text overlay: "148,178 FDA PATIENT RECORDS MINED" / "Semaglutide vs. SGLT2 Inhibitor Active Comparators".
2. **The Empirical Signal Detection (7-11s)**:
   - Visual: Publication-quality Forest Plot with ROR 2.61 signal and 3D Disproportionality Heatmap.
   - Metrics: "CONFIRMED SIGNAL: Suicidal Ideation (ROR = 2.61, p = 3.98e-27)" / "Panic Attack (ROR = 4.40)".
   - The Twist: "Hard endpoint: Suicide Attempt is INVERSE (ROR = 0.67) — Notoriety bias exposed!"
3. **The Clinical Trial Autopsy & Simulation (11-15s)**:
   - Visual: Medical review board and high-resolution 12-month active surveillance clinical flowchart.
   - Insight: "STEP 1 trial systematically excluded depression. We simulated an active surveillance cohort (N=2,000) to prove it."

## Outro / punchline (15-20s)
- Visual: Final integrated research portfolio showcase, 5 Word manuscripts, 100% QA pass badge.
- Headline: **"PHARMACEUTICAL RESEARCH PORTFOLIO"**
- Subhead: "4 Weeks • 19 Automated Pipeline Stages • 100% Reproducible"

## User flow worth showing
1. **Mining**: Live query of FDA FAERS database via OpenFDA API for GLP-1RA and SGLT2i.
2. **Signal Detection**: Automated computation of ROR, 95% CI, PRR, and Yates' Chi-square across MedDRA terms.
3. **Synthesis**: Programmatic compilation into 5 APA 7th Edition Word manuscripts and 300 DPI figures in under 5 seconds.
