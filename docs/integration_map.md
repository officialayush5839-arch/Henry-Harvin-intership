```

                    ↓

```

## 3. Consistency Audit Across Deliverables

1. **Terminology Standardization**:
   - The primary drug is consistently referred to as **Semaglutide** (brand names: Ozempic, Wegovy, Rybelsus).
   - The comparator class is consistently specified as **Sodium-Glucose Cotransporter-2 (SGLT2) Inhibitors** (specifically Empagliflozin, Dapagliflozin, Canagliflozin).
   - Adverse events adhere strictly to Medical Dictionary for Regulatory Activities (**MedDRA**) 27.0 Preferred Terms: *Depression, Depressed Mood, Suicidal Ideation, Suicide Attempt, Anxiety, Insomnia, Panic Attack*.

2. **Hypothesis Coherence**:
   - In Week 1, $H_0$ posited $\text{ROR} \le 1.0$ and $H_1$ posited $\text{ROR} > 1.0$.
   - In Week 2, empirical testing rejected $H_0$ for Suicidal Ideation ($\text{ROR} = 2.61$), Depressed Mood ($\text{ROR} = 2.11$), and Panic Attack ($\text{ROR} = 4.40$).
   - In Week 3, the prospective hypothesis translated this signal into hazard rates ($H_0: \text{HR} = 1.0$ vs. $H_1: \text{HR} > 1.0$).
   - In Week 4, the clinical trial critique established why pre-approval trials failed to challenge $H_0$ due to restrictive inclusion criteria.

3. **Data & Numerical Integrity**:
   - All Week 2 figures (1 through 8), tables, and descriptive stats match exactly with the raw FAERS data extractions (`100,912` semaglutide reports, `47,266` SGLT2i reports).
   - Week 3 power calculations ($N = 2,000$) directly reflect the baseline event rates and effect sizes observed in Weeks 1 and 2.
   - Week 4 trial statistics ($N = 1,961$, $14.9\%$ weight loss, $92.6\%$ completion) reflect the verified NEJM manuscript.
